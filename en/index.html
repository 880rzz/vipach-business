The point

The first impression comes before the first conversation.

The current state

In many companies the strategy lives mainly in the CEO’s head. Website, visual presence, proof points, structured data and language are not aligned.

The result: the company is harder to scale, harder to transfer and easier to undervalue in an international context.

A digital entity is the company’s institutionalised, verifiable digital form.

What can an AI agent discover about your business?

AI research may precede your next business conversation.

Before a meeting, a potential client, partner or investor can use an AI assistant to examine public records, leadership, past statements, coverage and contradictions. This does not happen before every deal, but the tools are widely accessible.

Wayback Machine

The Internet Archive’s Wayback Machine preserves snapshots of websites captured at different dates. A removed or revised page may remain visible in an older archived version. Coverage is incomplete, and every archived claim requires a date, source and context.

Social media · Reddit · AI

Older public posts, comments, forums and Reddit discussions may surface. An employee’s personal comment is not automatically a corporate position. AI can also misidentify people or companies, so evidence and attribution matter.

Addressing your public digital footprint early helps reduce avoidable risk. Copies and archives can persist, but it is inaccurate to say that every detail becomes permanently embedded in LLMs. VIPACH identifies substantiated issues and helps prioritise and implement remedies when self-service is not enough.

What does this mean in practice?

A website is only one part of a company's digital presence. We examine whether the information available about the business is clear, consistent and verifiable.

Why is a good-looking website not enough?

Prospective clients, partners and search systems may consult company registers, professional profiles, publications and independent references as well as your website. Conflicting names, services, locations or claims can create uncertainty.

What does the audit examine?

We assess how clearly the business can be identified, the consistency of public information, the clarity of its services, the quality of supporting evidence and relevant technical signals. Depending on the agreed scope, this can include canonical URLs, language versions, structured data and indexability.

What will we receive?

A documented assessment, identified gaps and a prioritised set of recommendations. Verified issues are distinguished from potential risks and questions requiring additional business information.

What happens after the audit?

Your team can implement the recommendations independently or request further support. Priorities are based on business relevance, effort and dependencies.

Can you guarantee Google rankings or AI recommendations?

No. Search and AI platforms control their own results. The work improves clarity, verifiability, technical accessibility and consistency; rankings and recommendations cannot be guaranteed.

Why does this matter before selling a company?

A buyer needs to understand more than financial performance: the business's track record, market position, relationships and operating capabilities also matter. If years of work are poorly documented online or public sources contradict one another, digital due diligence can become harder. The audit identifies information gaps and evidence that can be substantiated. It does not replace financial, legal or transaction due diligence.

Why audit a business after an acquisition?

After a change of ownership, old and new names, contacts, service descriptions and business profiles may coexist. An audit identifies what needs updating so the new leadership can present the company's continuity and direction clearly and credibly.

Why is years of work missing from AI answers?

A well-established company may still have little reliable, machine-readable public evidence about its work. An AI assistant may omit it, confuse it with another business or generate inaccurate claims. We examine which public sources support the facts, what is missing and where accuracy can improve. AI answers vary; a single test cannot establish lasting visibility or invisibility.

Does a vulnerability audit reveal everything?

No. Within an agreed scope and methodology, it identifies verifiable inconsistencies, unsupported claims, ambiguous descriptions and accessibility issues in a company's digital presence. Findings are linked to sources, assessed by risk and prioritised for remediation. It is not a substitute for a comprehensive security, legal or financial audit.
