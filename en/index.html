Audit method and further options

What do we check, and how?

We compare your website, search results and public sources against the agreed questions. Where relevant, we also check language versions, structured data and indexability. A single AI answer is not evidence of lasting visibility. We check conclusions against their sources.

What happens after the consultation?

We agree the scope, then send a separate proposal. The audit and any implementation start only when you accept the relevant proposal.
